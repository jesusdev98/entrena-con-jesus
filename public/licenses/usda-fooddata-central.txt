Entrena con Jesús selected records, added Spanish display labels and preparation
categories, and selected published kcal by application policy: nutrient 2048,
then 2047, then 1008. This precedence is not a USDA requirement. Nutrient 1062
is kJ and is not used as kcal. Published values are preserved per 100 g edible
portion, without rounding, serving-volume conversion, cooking conversion, or
4/4/9 recalculation. Original descriptions and FDC IDs accompany every entry.
Spanish labels/selection are project adaptations, not USDA translations or
endorsement. Raw archives and nested input-food/sample data are not bundled.
